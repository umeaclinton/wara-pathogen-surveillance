Microbial Safety & Virulence Phenotypes of Unpasteurized Wara Cheese

Epidemiological bio-surveillance platform analyzing 40 confirmed bacterial isolates from Ojoo Main Market, Ibadan. Measuring phenotypic virulence, in vitro beta-hemolysis, and the equilibrium between indigenous probiotic Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB) and foodborne enteric contaminants.
